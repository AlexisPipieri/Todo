No accounts, no sync, no bloat. Just a clean list that's always one click away, stored locally on your Mac.

Deadlines

Type @tomorrow or @monday inline and MenuTodo understands. Due dates show as a subtle pill on your task.

Projects

Assign color-coded tags with #work or #personal inline. Create new projects on the fly without leaving the input.

Buckets

Split tasks between Today and Anytime. Focus on what matters now, park the rest for later — move tasks between buckets with one tap.

Auto-rollover

Unfinished tasks carry over automatically at midnight. Nothing falls through the cracks — no manual work required.
